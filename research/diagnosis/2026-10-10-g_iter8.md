## Lens: ECONOMY

ECONOMY: why the g_iter8 economy (src/c_aura2, sub 129) stalls from about r200 against strong teams. The analysis ran 2026-10-10 06:00-06:50 PDT and was read-only: no games were run and no repo files were touched. Data: g_iter8 replica panel 3233-3242 (100 games, created 05:14 PDT; 33 strong losses). Sources: matches/<id>/extract/{games,timeline,hq,trips,deaths,engagements,robots,tele_states,census}.csv and the engine .map23 files via diag/map.py. 'Strong' means vrangr1, jmerle, awesomelemonade, pranayagra, georgezhang and NotLLeon. 'Raid maps' means Cat, Cornucopia, DefaultMap, Hah, IslandHopping, Maze and ReverseFunnel (20 of the 33 strong losses).

Short answer: the stall has two different causes, depending on the map.
- On the raid maps, our carriers die about twice as often as theirs under the same home pressure. This starts before the launcher gap opens. Most of these deaths happen at wells beyond the HQ's vision and come in series, because a raid on a well is never shared with the team.
- On Forest (and MassiveL), carriers are plentiful but the mana is not. On Forest our mana is capped by a single 6-slot well, and HQ (11,48) receives no deliveries at all for 400 rounds.
- The banked adamantium at besieged HQs and the low production at free HQs are consequences of these two causes.

What still holds from g_iter6: the production/death split of the carrier gap, the 4x trip death rate, flee trips ending in death, and Forest/MassiveL well access. The anchor leak is fixed.

### Proposed arms

- **c_rep1** (small): Base g_iter8 (src/c_aura2). Changes go in Carrier, Comms and C only; Launcher and HQ are unchanged.

(1) Carrier.survive(): when threat != null, set new statics threatLoc = threat.location and threatRound = G.round.

(2) Carrier.run(), right after reportEnemies(): if Comms.canWrite && threatLoc != null && G.round - threatRound <= C.REPORT_AGE (32), call Comms.reportEnemy(threatLoc) and set threatLoc = null. This is a normal ENEMY sighting with the current stamp. reportEnemy already refreshes a sighting of the same cluster or takes the oldest slot. The flee itself is unchanged.

(3) Raided-well skip, with no hold. A well is raided when Comms.nearestEnemy(well, 1) (age 1 stamp or less) lies within r2 C.RAID_SKIP_R2 (36) of it.
(a) At the top of gather(): if well != null, the carrier is more than r2 8 from it, and the well is raided, set well = null and crowdTurns = 0.
(b) When well == null, pick in this order:
- the nearest known well of the role (shared slots plus MapMem seen wells) that is not raided;
- else the nearest well of any type that is not raided, with the role switched for this trip only (flag offRole; deliver() sets well = null at the deposit and restores the role);
- else the nearest well of the role anyway. Never idle: no 'W' hold, unlike c_flee3.
(c) switchWell() skips raided candidates.
Read the 4 ENEMY slots once per turn (cache them).
