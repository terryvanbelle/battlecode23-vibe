Short answer: our economy has two parts. HQs under siege stop producing. The free HQs make about half what the opponents' free HQs make. Our carrier fleet stops growing at 14-16 from r150, while the opponent's grows from 21 to 42. At r400 the gap of 23 carriers splits into 14.8 fewer built and 8.2 more killed. Carrier deaths also cut collection per carrier, because 26-29% of our carrier turns are spent on trips that end in death.

The part we control directly is behaviour around contact:
- once a carrier has fled, it walks back to the same well and dies on 46-72% of those trips;
- 34% of our carriers are built while a fight is on near their HQ (opponents: 5%), and 40% of those die within 20 rounds.

Two other effects are mostly consequences or map-specific:
- passive adamantium piles up, unspendable, at besieged HQs;
- mana wells are lost to the enemy army.

What still holds from the g_iter3 diagnosis:
- The production gap holds: carriers built r200-500 are 5.9/4.9/4.8 per 100 rounds against 11.7/11.8/11.5, up from g_iter3's 4.5/2.6/3.2.
- The idle adamantium bank holds and c_anc3's bank cap did not change it.
- Carrier deaths per trip still run about 4x the opponents' (35% vs 7-9%).
- The Forest queue and the doubled well waits hold.
- The anchor drain is fixed.

### Proposed arms

- **c_flee2** (medium): Carriers break off a trip once they have met an enemy, and route away from besieged HQs. Base: g_iter6 (src/c_anc3).
(1) Carrier.survive(): whenever a threat is found (the flee branch), set threatLoc = threat.location and threatRound = G.round. If `well` is within r2 C.AVOID_R2 (64) of threatLoc, set well = null and crowdTurns = 0. If rc.getWeight() >= C.FLEE_BANK_LOAD (10), set returning = true, so a partial load is banked instead of being carried back to the well.
(2) New Carrier.pickWell(role), used by gather() whenever well == null, in place of nearer(Comms.nearestWell, MapMem.nearestSeenWell). Candidates are the shared wells (Comms 5..20) plus the seen wells (MapMem), scored by dist2 from here, +400 for the wrong type. Skip a candidate within r2 C.AVOID_R2 of threatLoc while G.round - threatRound <= C.THREAT_MEMORY (40). Also skip one within r2 C.AVOID_R2 of a Comms sighting at most 1 stamp old (read the 4 ENEMY slots once). Take the best of the role's type, else the best of any type with the role switched for this trip only (restore it at deposit). If none is left, state 'W': moveTwice(HQState.safest(G.here)) and hold adjacent to it, re-picking each turn, for at most C.HOLD_MAX (15) turns; after that, take the nearest well ignoring the filter.
(3) HQState.safest(from): the nearest HQ whose Comms pressed bit (AD_FLAGS bit 4+i; add Comms.pressed(i)) is clear, falling back to the nearest HQ. deliver() uses safest() when the nearest HQ is pressed and safest() is within 2x its distance. survive()'s toward-home branch uses safest() in place of HQState.nearest().
