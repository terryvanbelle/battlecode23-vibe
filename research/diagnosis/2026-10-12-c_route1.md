# Experimental carrier route recovery

`c_route1` (`471eafaef776`) is an isolated c_mend2 copy. Only `C.java` and `Carrier.java` change after package
normalization. It repairs outbound gather routes that keep making legal field detours without improving their
starting wave. HQ production, well selection, communication, navigation primitives and launchers retain Mend2.
The active submission remains c_mend2/145. This candidate has not passed the official full suite or game screen.

Outside distance squared 8 of its well, a gathering carrier observes an active, ready, exact-target field once
per eligible round. After four eligible rounds without a better valid wave, it follows the existing bug hand
and suppresses field selection before both movement calls until collection. An exact target/field epoch change,
delivery, fleeing, anchoring or scouting clears the memory. No new RNG draw is added. Admission requires 6,500
bytecodes before looking up a wave and 4,000 afterward; these are provisional guards, pending metering.

The cumulative `rr` indicator counts recoveries. Its suffix is emitted only with at least 600 bytecodes left;
deferred observations are unknown. The existing replay reader accepts the counter. Counter formatting and the
complete turn need price/headroom checks too.

The first official native mechanical fixture passed 320 cases / 35,424 checks. Actual 30 kg collection and HQ
deposit completed in 40/40 locked-policy groups against 29/40 baseline groups. The later actual-source prototype
uses three separate JVM/class trees: enabled 48/48 deposits, baseline and disabled 37/48. All 48 disabled native
traces match Mend2; all 48 enabled paths match before the trigger. Eight real same-type well switches test memory
reset before and after recovery. The official actual-source wrapper is prepared and remains a required check.

All 54 targeted guard/counter checks pass locally. Compiled disabled code preserves every original Carrier
method and class initializer after symbolic normalization. Native host fixtures use synthetic `Clock` budgets
and do not measure instruction safety. They also exclude full `Carrier.run` priority and counter formatting.

One tested temporary AD blocker makes deposit ten rounds later, 54 to 64. Rescuing the AD hotspot alone does not
explain Forest's larger Mana-site deficit. Keep this experimental until the official suite, measured cost and
fixed own-versus-own delivery/screen games show safe, productive behavior. Measure collected and deposited Mana,
first-collection age, `rr`, stalls, deaths and well distribution alongside wins; a lower stall count alone cannot
accept the arm. Any overrun, new exception, disabled-control drift or failed delivery blocks it.

Compile-time zero dose is `C.CARRIER_ROUTE_RECOVERY=false`; initial threshold is four eligible no-progress rounds,
with eight as the predeclared conservative extension if temporary-obstacle regressions dominate. Do not combine
it with the independent crowded-well exploration or healing changes before positive delivery evidence.
