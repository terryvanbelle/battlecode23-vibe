A separate exploration-policy experiment should replace the search target choice inside the empty-Mana/no-known-alternative episode. It should preserve the original crowd trigger, roles, HQ decisions, navigation, partial-cargo behavior, survival priorities and twelve-round retreat deadline. Extending the deadline alone does not address the current target at the map centre.

The proposed mechanism is coverage of unknown map regions, using only `MapMem.tile`, map dimensions, known allied HQ locations, the carrier location and known wells. It never uses Forest well coordinates or opponent behavior. A small deterministic lattice of candidate waypoints, spaced six tiles apart, gives broad coverage at ordinary carrier vision. Select an unknown, non-wall waypoint beyond the rejected well's collection neighborhood, prefer short reachable routes, and restrict each waypoint to a fixed Chebyshev distance from at least one known allied HQ. A carrier-specific deterministic tie order distributes carriers without changing role ratios. Reaching or sensing a waypoint advances to another unknown waypoint; already scanned tiles persist across episodes, so repeated bounded trips seek new regions instead of repeating the same centre-directed corridor. Known new Mana still takes priority.

Before choosing constants or writing a candidate, price the bounded scan and compare a zero arm on the official instrumenter. Require explicit reserve checks before scan and movement, limit candidate evaluations, and retain the best completed waypoint if the scan stops. This changes decisions under finite budgets and must be reported as such. A valid target must be on the map; known walls, already seen targets and exhausted candidate sets must return to the existing fallback without exceptions.

Preregister the first structural dose with the existing twelve-round exclusion, and record per episode target, movement, newly seen tiles, known Mana, first new-site pickup and retreat. The Forest unknown/shared controls must be retained. Absence of centre discovery is a decisive negative result for that dose, not permission to widen it during the same test. A longer dose, alternate waypoint score or different safety radius would be a separate frozen experiment justified by its measured search coverage and travel cost.

Fixtures should cover all-visible/no-frontier maps, narrow walls/current corridors, cloud-limited sensing, unknown versus known alternative Mana, partially loaded carriers, role change, expired episodes, low budgets and multiple HQs. Native full-bot own-vs-own games must confirm zero-arm identity and real pickup-to-HQ transfer with normal overrun/exception/near-miss checks. A structural fixture does not establish game strength or that the motivating live games lacked shared centre knowledge.

This is a preregistration draft. No policy source, game job or training screen has been created or launched.
