**The funnel** (since 2026-10-11; earlier attempts were judged by calibration cells against field bots, 20-game head-to-heads and ladder trials, listed under "Other local tests"):
1. **Local h2h**: every map, both sides (206 games) against the incumbent, first with map seeds, then random seeds. *Signal* = maps won on both sides minus maps lost on both sides.
2. **Screen** (`tools/screen.py`): (a) basics vs examplefuncsplayer; (b) head-to-head on the 10 panel maps, both sides, against the validated build (PASS at 11/20, BORDERLINE at 9); (c) the archetype roster, paired with the incumbent (net; "info" = no gating archetype); (d) overruns, exceptions and self-deaths over all candidate games.
3. **Panel trial**: 100 games on the replica against 10 field bots; *net* = games gained minus games lost against the validated build's panel (`tools/paired.py`; most games are identical).
4. **Outcome**: accepted (submission id), rejected, dropped before the next stage, parked or subsumed.

Totals: 94 other (parked, subsumed, not tested), 42 dropped before the next stage, 27 rejected on the panel, 17 accepted, 13 rejected at the screen, 3 baseline (196 attempts).
